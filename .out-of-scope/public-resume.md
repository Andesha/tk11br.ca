# Public Resume

This site does not publish a directly downloadable resume or CV.

## Why this is out of scope

A public resume would give away a document intended for targeted professional contexts, without the surrounding conversation or intent that usually makes it useful. It would also be easy for the file to drift out of date, leaving visitors with a stale or misleading version of Tyler's current experience.

The preferred path is for visitors to use the existing contact routes and external profiles instead. Those are easier to keep current and allow resume sharing to happen when it is actually relevant.
