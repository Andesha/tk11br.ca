EEGStudyFlow is the name I use for the workflow patterns that sit around an EEG analysis project: where raw recordings live, how preprocessing state is tracked, how quality-control decisions are recorded, and how analysis-ready outputs are handed off.

This page is intentionally brief for now, but the project goal is simple: make EEG studies easier to reproduce without asking every researcher to keep the whole pipeline in their head.
